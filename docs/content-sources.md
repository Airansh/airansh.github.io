- Current role uses Emerging Technology Strategist, as given in the general DOCX and earlier iBioFoundry resume. Older titles vary. Simplex and Chenoa use the primary resume's titles; LinkedIn variants are not silently treated as additional roles.
- 1,000+ agent users across 8+ units; 147 Gateway pilot participants; 400+ workshop participants; 1,500+ HawkAI learners are distinct measures, not additive unique audiences.
- The DSHB resume's 12 departments/200+ users and 1,600+ learners are not merged with the other scopes.
- No deployment topology, security certification, SLA, database schema, API contract, or production-performance benchmark is inferred for proprietary systems.
- Hospital demo: current README overrides older broad claims about secure authentication, completed appointment booking, and HL7 compliance. Server-side auth limitations and the absent booking endpoint are explicit.
- Voting is educational and team-built. No election certification is claimed. Resume supports GitLab CI/SonarQube configuration; current README also documents GitHub Actions.
- EvalForge: OpenAI adapter exists but is not live-verified. Anthropic/Gemini runs and small-sample/same-family judge caveats are retained. Test counts are attributed, not claimed as rerun.
- VR engagement: 38% is resume-reported; its metric definition and study protocol are not available. No causal claims beyond the supplied record are added.
- No bat-collision reduction, AR transcription accuracy, photo processing multiplier, WordPress traffic multiplier, or ambiguous 90% manufacturing efficiency figure is published without clearer measurement context.
- IdeaStorm: “recognition” is used because supplied versions differ on winner versus third-place wording.
- IoT review citation is resume-supplied. The Journal of Behavioral Education manuscript remains “submitted.”
- ITS article URLs and the Electronic Imaging program URL were extracted from the UW PDF. Network policy returned 403 on these hosts; independent publisher verification remains pending. They are not represented as verified links.
- Seattle and San Diego relocation statements belong to targeted resumes and are excluded from general site copy.
- The AR application and BackdropWizard remain separate projects; the supplied materials do not establish that they are one system.
